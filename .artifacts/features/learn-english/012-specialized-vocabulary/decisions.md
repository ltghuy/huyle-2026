# Decisions: Specialized vocabulary sets

Decisions on gray areas and ambiguities resolved during discussion.

## Resolved

### Where set words come from

- **Question:** Should a set reuse course words, hold only new specialized words, or both?
- **Decision:** Both. A set is a curated list: course words that fit (about 750 sit in the Công nghệ, Công việc and Kinh doanh topics across levels) plus new specialized words drafted, reviewed and approved like other content.
- **Rationale:** Reusing course words keeps one entry per word, so learned progress carries over. New words cover the jargon the CEFR lists lack.
- **Caveats:** Drafting must check new words against the course to avoid duplicates (AC-016).

### How a learner studies a set

- **Question:** Browse only, practice from the set, or follow the set as a track in the daily lesson?
- **Decision:** Follow it as a track: Bài học hôm nay takes new words from the followed set, and learned words join Ôn tập.
- **Rationale:** Words only stick through the review schedule, and the daily lesson is how new words enter it today.

### Size and choice of the first sets

- **Question:** Which sets and how big?
- **Decision:** Three sets of about 100 words: Công nghệ thông tin, Tuyển dụng, Hành chính văn phòng.
- **Rationale:** Owner's choice (2026-10-29).

### Placement

- **Question:** A tab on Từ vựng or a separate page?
- **Decision:** A "Chuyên ngành" option next to the level tabs on Từ vựng.
- **Rationale:** Keeps one place for words, with the same cards, search and word dialog.

## Tentative

### Set words replace level words in the lesson

- **Question:** When a set is followed, do its words replace the lesson's level words or come alongside them?
- **Tentative Decision:** Replace. The lesson's new words come from the set, with the same daily count and time budget, topped up with level words only when the set runs short. Level words come back once the set is finished or unfollowed.
- **Revisit when:** Learners want both at once; a split (for example half set, half level) would be the next step.

### One followed set at a time

- **Question:** Can a learner follow several sets?
- **Tentative Decision:** One at a time; following another set replaces it.
- **Revisit when:** More sets exist and learners ask to mix them.

### No CEFR level for specialized words

- **Question:** Should new specialized words get a CEFR level?
- **Tentative Decision:** No. They show the set's name instead of a level, and they don't appear in the level lists of Từ vựng. Course words in a set keep their level.
- **Revisit when:** Learners find set words too hard early on; then the set order (easier words first) may need a difficulty tag.
