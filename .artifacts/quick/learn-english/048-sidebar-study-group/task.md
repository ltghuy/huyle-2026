# Quick Task: Group the study pages in the sidebar

## What

The desktop sidebar puts "Từ vựng", "Ngữ pháp" and "Động từ bất quy tắc" under one collapsible group, "Thư viện", which is closed by default.

## Files

- apps/learn-english/src/components/shell/nav.ts
- apps/learn-english/src/components/shell/app-sidebar.tsx
- apps/learn-english/src/components/tour/tour-steps.ts
- apps/learn-english/src/components/ui/collapsible.tsx (generated by shadcn)

## Expected Outcome

- The sidebar shows a "Thư viện" row in place of the three items, followed by the other items in their usual order.
- Clicking the row opens or closes the group. It opens on its own when the current page is one of the three.
- When the sidebar is collapsed to icons, the three items show as icons like before, because a sub-list can't show there.
- The mobile bottom bar and the "Thêm" sheet stay as they are.
- During the first-visit tour, a step for one of the three points at the "Thư viện" row while the group is closed.
