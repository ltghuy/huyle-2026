# Quick Task: Previous / next word in the vocabulary dialog

## What

To read the next word, learners had to close the word dialog and open another card. Add a joined previous / next control next to "Đóng", with the position ("3/1164") between the arrows. The ← and → keys do the same. The arrows step through the list the word was opened from: the level and topic, or the vocabulary set, honouring "Chỉ hiện từ chưa học". Extends feature 001 AC-007 (word dialog).
